(FAQ — 05)Before we get started

Before we get started.

A few things clients ask most often. Still wondering about something? Send it over and I'll answer within a day.

Do you write the copy, or do we?+

Either works. I can write conversion-focused copy tailored to your brand and goals, or polish and structure content you provide. Most projects are a collaboration.

What happens after launch?+

You get a tidy handover, documentation, and 30 days of free support. From there we can set up an ongoing retainer to keep improving results.

How long does a project take?+

A focused landing page runs 3–4 weeks; a full site 6–10. I take on a limited number of projects at once so yours always has my attention.

What if I'm not happy with the result?+

We work in clear milestones with revision rounds at each. You always know where things stand, and nothing ships until you're genuinely happy with it.
